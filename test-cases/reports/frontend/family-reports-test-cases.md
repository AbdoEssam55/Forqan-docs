# Family reports hub — Frontend Test Cases

## Metadata

- Project: `alforqan`
- Feature: `reports`
- Layer: `frontend`
- Routes: ``/{locale}/{tenantSlug}/reports``, ``/{locale}/{tenantSlug}/reports/memorization``, ``/{locale}/{tenantSlug}/reports/session/{sessionId}/student/{studentId}``, ``/{locale}/{tenantSlug}/sard/{sardSessionId}/review``
- APIs: `GET /API/V01/FamilyReports/children`, `GET /API/V01/FamilyReports/halaqat`, `POST /API/V01/FamilyReports/list`
- Related code: `src/app/[locale]/(tenant)/[tenantSlug]/reports/page.tsx`, `src/app/[locale]/(tenant)/[tenantSlug]/reports/reports-hub-content.tsx`, `src/features/alforqan/reports/family-reports-explorer.tsx`, `src/features/alforqan/reports/family-report-card.tsx`, `src/features/alforqan/reports/use-reports-hub-role.ts`, `src/features/alforqan/config/tenant-nav.ts`, `src/features/alforqan/components/tenant-navigation.tsx`, `src/components/ui/table-pagination.tsx`, `src/lib/auth/tenant-role-claim-rules.ts`, `src/components/users/user-roles-and-claims-tab.tsx`, `src/components/users/user-claims-manager.tsx`
- Related docs: `docs/technical/tenant/family-reports-guide.md`, `docs/explanation/tenant/reports-explained.md`

## Scope

- Reports nav visibility for students (`ViewOwnReportsClaim`) and parents (`ViewReportsClaim`).
- Role-aware `/reports` landing: student "My reports" explorer vs parent "Children's reports" explorer vs staff category tiles.
- Parents see a child filter and child name on every card; all-children view groups by child then date.
- Staff sub-routes blocked for parents without staff report claims.
- Family users browse session hifz/revision and sard results by date; cards deep-link to existing detail pages.

## Test pages

- `/{locale}/{tenantSlug}/reports`
- `/{locale}/{tenantSlug}/reports/memorization`
- `/{locale}/{tenantSlug}/reports/session/{sessionId}/student/{studentId}`
- `/{locale}/{tenantSlug}/sard/{sardSessionId}/review`
